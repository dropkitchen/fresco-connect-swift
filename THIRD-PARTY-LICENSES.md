# Third-Party Licences

`FrescoConnectKit.xcframework` **redistributes no third-party components.** It compiles in no third-party
code, so there are no third-party licence notices for this artifact to carry.

The frameworks it links — RecipeEdit, Pantry and KitchenOS — are separate binaries delivered to you in
their own right, each carrying its own licence and its own attribution file. Their notices travel with
them, not with this artifact.

Generated from the resolved dependency graph on every release. Components used only to build or test
FrescoConnectKit are excluded, because they are not present in the artifact.
